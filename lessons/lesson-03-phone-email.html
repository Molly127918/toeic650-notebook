第 03 課｜電話與 Email Phone & Email

接客戶電話、回覆詢問信、把檔案轉寄給主管，每天的工作有一大半都在電話和信箱之間來回，多益閱讀也超愛考 Email。

📌 學習目標

  • 能看懂電話與 Email 情境的 10 個高頻單字
  • 能用 Could you 和 Would you mind 正確提出禮貌請求
  • 能用「先看題目、再找段落」的方法解 Part 7 的 Email 閱讀題

📝 核心單字

單字 詞性 中文 例句
inquiry n. 詢問；查詢 Thank you for your inquiry about our delivery service. 感謝您詢問我們的配送服務。
reply v. 回覆 Could you reply to the client's email by noon? 你可以在中午前回覆客戶的信嗎？
attach v. 附加（檔案） I have attached the price list to this email. 我已經把價目表附在這封信裡了。
forward v. 轉寄 Would you mind forwarding this message to Mr. Lee? 你介意把這封訊息轉寄給李先生嗎？
confirm v. 確認 Please call us to confirm your appointment. 請來電確認您的預約。
available adj. 有空的；可取得的 Ms. Wang is not available right now. May I take a message? 王小姐現在不方便接電話，要幫您留言嗎？
extension n. （電話）分機 You can reach the sales department at extension 205. 您可以撥分機 205 找業務部。
voicemail n. 語音留言 I left a voicemail for the supplier this morning. 我今天早上有在供應商的語音信箱留言。
regarding prep. 關於 I am writing regarding the order I placed last week. 我寫這封信是關於我上週下的訂單。
get back to phr. （稍後）回覆某人 I'll check the schedule and get back to you this afternoon. 我查一下行程，下午再回覆你。

✏️ 文法重點

禮貌請求 Could you / Would you mind

想請同事或客戶幫忙時，比起直接說 Send me the file，用 Could you 或 Would you mind 會客氣很多。最大的差別在後面接的動詞：Could you 後面接原形動詞，Would you mind 後面接 V-ing。另外要注意，Would you mind 字面上是「你介意嗎？」，所以答應幫忙時要說 Not at all（一點也不介意）。

Could you + 原形動詞 …?
Would you mind + V-ing …?
  • Could you send me the meeting notes by Friday? 你可以在週五前把會議筆記寄給我嗎？（Could you + 原形動詞 send）
  • Would you mind calling the customer back after lunch? 你介意午餐後回電給那位客戶嗎？（Would you mind + V-ing calling）

常見錯誤

錯誤 Would you mind to forward the invoice to me?

正確 Would you mind forwarding the invoice to me?

🎯 題型技巧

Part 7 單篇閱讀（Email）

讀一篇職場文章（這課以 Email 為主），回答 2 到 4 題關於主旨、細節或寫信者請求的問題。

解題技巧

  1. 先看信件開頭的寄件人、收件人和主旨（Subject），馬上知道「誰寫給誰、為了什麼事」。
  2. 問寫信目的的題目，答案通常在第一段，常見句型是 I am writing regarding / to …。
  3. 問「寫信者要求對方做什麼」時，直接去找 Could you、Would you mind、Please 開頭的句子。

示範題

To: Kevin Lin
From: Amy Chen
Subject: Order #4512

Dear Mr. Lin,
I am writing regarding the office chairs we ordered last week. We received only 15 chairs, but our order was for 20. Could you check your records and let me know when the other five will arrive? I have attached a copy of our order form. Thank you for your help.
Best regards,
Amy Chen

What does Ms. Chen ask Mr. Lin to do?
(A) Send her a new order form
(B) Give her a refund
(C) Tell her when the rest of the chairs will be delivered
(D) Call her at her office

解題步驟：

  1. 先讀題目：問 Ms. Chen「要求」Mr. Lin 做什麼，所以去找請求句。
  2. 信中找到 Could you check your records and let me know when the other five will arrive?
  3. the other five will arrive 被換句話說成 the rest of the chairs will be delivered。(A) 訂單是 Ms. Chen 自己附上的；(B)、(D) 信中都沒提。答案是 (C)。

❓ 小測驗

1. Would you mind ______ the sales report to everyone on the team?

  • (A) forward
  • (B) to forward
  • (C) forwarding
  • (D) forwarded
點擊看答案

正確答案：(C)

Would you mind 後面一定接 V-ing，所以是 forwarding。(A) 原形動詞是接在 Could you 後面；(B) 不可以接 to + V；(D) 過去式在這裡文法不通。

2. Could you ______ the date of the interview with Ms. Park before Friday?

  • (A) confirm
  • (B) confirming
  • (C) to confirm
  • (D) confirms
點擊看答案

正確答案：(A)

Could you 後面接原形動詞，所以選 confirm（確認）。(B) V-ing 是 Would you mind 的用法；(C) 不需要 to；(D) 助動詞 could 後面不加 s。

3. I am writing ______ the invoice you sent us on May 3. Some of the prices seem to be incorrect.

  • (A) available
  • (B) regarding
  • (C) extension
  • (D) attach
點擊看答案

正確答案：(B)

I am writing regarding … 是 Email 開頭說明「寫信目的」的固定說法，regarding 是介系詞，意思是「關於」。(A) available 是形容詞；(C) extension 是名詞「分機」；(D) attach 是動詞，放在這裡都不通。

🌟 本課重點整理

  • inquiry、attach、forward、regarding 是 Email 題的必考單字。
  • Could you 後面接原形動詞，Would you mind 後面接 V-ing。
  • 回答 Would you mind…? 時，願意幫忙要說 Not at all。
  • Part 7 Email 題先看寄件人、收件人和主旨，寫信目的通常在第一段。
  • 問「對方被要求做什麼」，就去找 Could you、Would you mind、Please 的句子。
